# Connect shapes

After you add shapes to the canvas, connect them to show the relationship or flow between them. You can connect shapes that are already on the canvas, or add a new shape and connect it to an existing one. In both cases, you use the blue directional arrows that appear when you point to a shape.

## Connect existing shapes

1. Open a diagram in the draw.io editor.
2. On the canvas, point to the shapes that you want to connect. Four blue directional arrows appear around the shape.
3. Click the arrow that points toward the other shape.

A connector appears between the two shapes.

## Connect a new shape

1. Open a diagram in the draw.io editor.
2. On the canvas, hover over the shape to which you want to add a new shape.
2. Hover over the arrow in the direction in which you want to add the new shape.
3. Select the required shape from the available options.

The new shape appears on the canvas, connected to the original shape. You can move the connected shapes while keeping their connectors attached.
