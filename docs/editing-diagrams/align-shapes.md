# Align shapes

You can align multiple shapes to arrange them neatly on the canvas.

## Procedure

1. Select at least two shapes that you want to align.
2. On the menu bar, click **Arrange**.
3. Click the required alignment option.

## Alignment options

| Option | Description |
|---|---|
| Left | Aligns the left edges of the selected shapes. |
| Center | Aligns the horizontal centers of the selected shapes. |
| Right | Aligns the right edges of the selected shapes. |
| Top | Aligns the top edges of the selected shapes. |
| Middle | Aligns the vertical centers of the selected shapes. |
| Bottom | Aligns the bottom edges of the selected shapes. |

The selected shapes are aligned according to the option you choose.
