# Format shapes

You can customize the appearance of shapes using the formatting panel in the draw.io editor.

## Procedure

1. Open a diagram in the draw.io editor.
2. Click the shape you want to format on the canvas.
3. To change the fill color, line color, and line style, in the right panel, open the **Style** tab
4. To customize the font, font size, alignment, and text color, click the **Text** tab
5. To adjust the shape's position, size, and rotation, click the **Arrange** tab.

The changes appear on the shape as you make them.
