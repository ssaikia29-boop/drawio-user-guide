# Move a shape

You can move shapes to reposition them on the canvas and organize your diagram.

## Procedure

1. Open a diagram in the draw.io editor.
2. Click the shape you want to move.
3. Click and hold the selected shape.
4. Drag it to the required position on the canvas.
5. Release the mouse button.

The shape moves to the new position. Any connectors attached to the shape remain connected.
