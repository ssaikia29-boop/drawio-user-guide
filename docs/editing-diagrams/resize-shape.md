# Resize a shape

You can resize shapes to adjust their dimensions on the canvas.

## Procedure

1. Open a diagram in the draw.io editor.
2. Click the shape you want to resize. Sizing handles appear around the selected shape.
3. Click and drag a sizing handle outward to increase the shape's size or inward to reduce it.
4. Release the mouse button when the shape reaches the required dimensions.

The shape is resized on the canvas.
