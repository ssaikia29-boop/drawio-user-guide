# Frequently Asked Questions

<details>
<summary><strong>Why is a connector not connecting to a shape?</strong></summary>
<p>Drag the connector endpoint until the connection point on the shape is highlighted.</p>
<p>For more information, see <a href="../creating-diagrams/connect-shapes.md">Connect shapes</a>.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>How do I bring an object to the front?</strong></summary>
<p>Select the object, open the <strong>Arrange</strong> menu, and select <strong>To Front</strong>.</p>
<p>For more information, see <a href="../editing-diagrams/arrange-objects.md">Arrange overlapping objects</a>.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>How do I align multiple shapes?</strong></summary>
<p>Select at least two shapes, open the <strong>Arrange</strong> menu, and choose the required alignment option.</p>
<p>For more information, see <a href="../editing-diagrams/align-shapes.md">Align shapes</a>.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Why is my exported diagram difficult to read?</strong></summary>
<p>Review the export settings before exporting. Adjust the settings to make sure the diagram is clear and readable.</p>
<p>For more information, see <a href="../managing-diagrams/export-diagram.md">Export a diagram</a>.</p>
</details>
